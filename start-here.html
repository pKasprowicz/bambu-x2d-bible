What does a slicer produce?

  • A 3D model
  • G-code toolpaths for the printer
  • Filament

The slicer converts a 3D model into layers and toolpaths (G-code) that the printer executes.

Conventions used

  • Units: metric first (mm, °C, g, mm/s). Imperial equivalents appear where useful.
  • Nozzle: unless stated, numbers assume a 0.4 mm nozzle on a hobby-grade FDM printer.
  • Numbers are starting points. Every printer, filament brand and room is different. Ranges are typical, not guaranteed — tune with the calibration chapter.
  • Bambu specifics come from public documentation. Check the official wiki for the latest firmware and hardware changes.
  • Spelling: American English.
